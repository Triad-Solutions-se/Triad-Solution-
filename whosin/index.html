Who's In

Group trip & party planning with expense splitting — by Triad Solutions, Gothenburg, Sweden.

Who's In helps friends and family organize a trip, party, or gathering: agree on a date, plan an itinerary together, share a map, split expenses, and settle up with Swish or Venmo deep links. An AI assistant helps with itineraries, dates, budgets, and activities.

Frequently asked

How does payment work?

The organizer pays a one-time session fee (an in-app purchase, priced by group size). Everything else — dinners, hotels, taxis — is tracked in the shared ledger, and members settle directly with each other via Swish or Venmo. No money moves through Who's In.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete account. Your personal data is erased; your contributions to shared ledgers are anonymized so other members' balances stay correct.

How do I export my data?

Settings → Data & Privacy → Export my data. You'll receive a JSON export plus a zip of your uploaded media by email.

How do I report inappropriate content?

Long-press any chat message or photo and tap Report. We respond to reports within 24 hours.
