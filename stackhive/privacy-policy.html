StackHive Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

StackHive ("the app") is developed by Triad Solutions ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why. We designed StackHive to keep data on your device wherever possible.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress — levels, stars, coins, streaks, settings and cosmetics — is saved on your device and, if you use iCloud, synced through your own iCloud account so it carries over to your other devices. It is not transmitted to us.

Gameplay analytics

To learn whether the game is fun and where players get stuck, the app sends us anonymous gameplay events: when the app is opened, when a level is started, won or lost (with the level number, mode, stars or score), and when the tutorial is finished. Each event includes the app version, your device's region setting and a random identifier created by the app on first launch.

That identifier is not your advertising identifier and is not linked to your name, Apple ID, Game Center account or any other app. We do not use this data for advertising or share it with anyone, and we cannot identify you from it. Deleting the app deletes the identifier; a reinstall starts a new one.

Advertising

StackHive shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, AdMob may access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and limited device and usage information. After your first level you are asked, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether to allow tracking. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. You can change this choice anytime in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Purchasing "Remove Ads" removes interstitial advertising.

Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Game Center

If you sign in to Apple Game Center, your leaderboard scores and Game Center identity are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We only submit scores (such as your best endless run or daily streak) to the leaderboards you see in the app.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device to remind you about the daily challenge. These are generated on-device and send no data to us.

Children

StackHive is suitable for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Decline ad tracking via the App Tracking Transparency prompt or iOS Settings.
  • Reset all local progress from the app's Settings screen.
  • Purchase "Remove Ads" to remove interstitial advertising.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "last updated" date.
