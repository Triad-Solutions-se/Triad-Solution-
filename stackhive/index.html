The game

Easy to pick up.
Hard to put down.

Daily challenge & streaks

A new shared board every day. Solve it to extend your streak — miss a day and it resets. Optional reminders keep you on track.

Endless mode

No timer, no finish line. Chase your best run and climb the Game Center leaderboards against players worldwide.

Levels, stars & coins

Work through handcrafted levels, earn up to three stars on each and collect coins along the way.

Play at your pace

No lives, no energy meters. StackHive waits for you — pick it up for a minute or settle in for an evening.

Make it yours

Spend coins on cosmetics, and tune sound, music, haptics and reduce-motion to play exactly the way you like.

Private by design

No account needed. Your progress lives on your device — we don't run servers that collect it.
