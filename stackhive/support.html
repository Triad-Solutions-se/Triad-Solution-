Frequently asked

I bought "Remove Ads" / coins but don't see them

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase and have a network connection.

How do the daily challenge and streak work?

A new shared board appears every day. Complete it to extend your streak; miss a day and the streak resets. Turn on Daily reminders in Settings so you don't forget.

How do I review the tutorial again?

Open Settings and tap How to play.

The effects are too intense

Turn on Reduce motion in Settings to soften screen shake and flashes. You can also toggle sound, music and haptics there.

How do I reset my progress?

Settings ▸ Reset progress. This clears levels, stars, coins and streaks (purchases are kept) and can't be undone.
